Settings
Focus & DND
Focus mode
Silences Tempo's nudges, hides sticky notes, and flips your OS's own do-not-disturb (GNOME/KDE, best-effort) — one switch.
Timed focus session
Drag the dial to set how long, then start. Turns itself back off when time's up.
Block distracting sites
Via /etc/hosts while focused. Needs your admin password once per toggle. Tap a site to toggle it — your picks are saved permanently, so you just pick from the list next time.

Focus tracking
Keystroke & mouse activity
Counts how often you're typing/clicking/moving the mouse, minute by minute, to build a focus score for each session. Never records which keys you press or where exactly you click — only rate. Stays on this device, never sent anywhere.
Camera presence coming soon
Would detect whether you're at your screen, entirely on-device — nothing recorded or sent anywhere. Not built yet: this just saves your preference for when it ships. The camera itself doesn't turn on.
